About Me

I work on the problems that still do not have good answers in robot learning.

The problem that keeps me up at night is not data. Data is important, but it is not what will solve robotics. What will solve it is when a robot can face a situation it was never trained on, reason through it, and find a better path than any demonstration ever showed it. That is what embodied reasoning is for, and it is still full of open problems I keep working on.

The future I am building toward is a robot brain you can download. One that works across any embodiment, the way Windows works across any hardware. You talk to it in plain language, it understands what you want, it figures out how to do it, and it keeps learning from every experience. That is where robot learning needs to go, and that is the direction every project I build points toward.
