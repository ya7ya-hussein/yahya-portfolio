About Me

I work on the problems that still do not have good answers in robot learning.

How do you build a policy that works across any embodiment, not just the robot it was trained on? How do you give a robot the ability to think through a scene it has never seen before committing to a move? How do you let a robot practice inside its own imagination before acting in the real world?

These are the open problems I build toward every day. My work sits at the intersection of deep RL and VLA fine-tuning. I work across the full End-to-End pipeline: from data collection to fine-tuning VLA models, including GR00T N1.7, π0/π0.5, and OpenVLA, with a solid understanding of their transformer, diffusion, and flow matching architectures.

If you are working on generalist robot policies, visuomotor policies, or embodied reasoning, I would love to connect.
