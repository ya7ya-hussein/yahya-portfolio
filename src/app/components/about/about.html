About Me

I believe physical AI will reshape the world. I'm building toward that.

The gap between simulation and the real world is where I work.

Most AI lives in servers. I'm interested in the kind that has to deal with a wet floor, a drifting sensor, and an obstacle that wasn't on the map. Embodied AI, robots that perceive, decide, and act in the physical world, is the hardest version of the intelligence problem, and the one worth solving.

I design end-to-end robot learning pipelines, from building simulation environments and shaping reward functions in Isaac Lab, through training policies with deep RL, behavior cloning, and diffusion models, to deploying on physical hardware through sim-to-real transfer. The goal is never just a policy that works in simulation. It is one that survives contact with the real world. I follow where the field is heading too: VLA architectures, world models, and the generalist robot policies that will eventually handle any task, in any environment, on any robot.
